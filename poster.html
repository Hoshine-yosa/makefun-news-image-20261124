WAR⚓

Ship hit in the Gulf of Finland

Zelensky confirmed the strike on a Russian ship; ally Norway urged caution. Russian attacks the same day killed 2 and injured 48 in Ukraine.

Saudi coalition strikes Hodeidah

The Saudi-led coalition hit the Houthi-held Red Sea port of Hodeidah; the Houthis warned they would strike Jeddah and all Saudi Red Sea ports.

UK warns Ukraine-aid firms of sabotage

Britain warned firms supplying Ukraine's military that they could be targeted by Russian sabotage, often low-tech arson through proxies.

ECONOMY📈

Huawei guilty in US secrets trial

A US jury found Huawei guilty of conspiring to steal trade secrets and bank fraud, but cleared it of racketeering conspiracy.

AI stocks slide, dragging US markets

Nvidia and Anthropic fell as doubts over the AI rally resurfaced; US Treasuries rose as investors sought safety.

Metals mixed after sell-off

Copper edged up and aluminium steadied after Tuesday's sharp metals sell-off, with traders awaiting US inflation data.

Dangote refinery to top Africa listings

Dangote's refinery will list in Lagos as Africa's biggest listed company by home market, far ahead of the No. 2 firm.

WORLD🌍

Landmine casualties hit 5-year high

Global landmine casualties rose to a five-year high, driven by Myanmar's civil war and Syria — where an 11-year-old girl was killed in the north-west.

Colombia car bomb injures 23

A car bomb outside a government building in Villavicencio injured at least 23 people; the president blamed a former FARC faction.

Hasina plans return on 16 December

Bangladesh's ex-premier Sheikh Hasina says she will return home on 16 December, after exile in Delhi since August 2024.
